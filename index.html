The container as launchpad

Field Sites

The container is the prototype space and the launchpad. Each mockup shows a day base where students can spend a whole day: shade, tables and power, using shelter that is already there (a barn's covered area, a stable porch, a beach palapa, a museum room, the plaza's arcade) or a tarp fly where there is none. A battery laser projector goes to every site, and each site uses a formation from the room.
